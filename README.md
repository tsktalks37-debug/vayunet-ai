# 🌿 VayuNet AI

### AI-Powered Hyperlocal Pollution Intelligence Platform for India

VayuNet AI is a climate intelligence platform designed to detect hidden and hyperlocal pollution hotspots across India by combining citizen reports, environmental sensor data, satellite signals, weather conditions, and AI-powered analysis.

The goal is to help cities and authorities identify pollution events earlier and coordinate faster responses.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Major Indian cities have air-quality monitoring systems, but these systems can miss highly localized pollution events such as:

- 🏭 Industrial emissions
- 🌾 Agricultural burning
- 🚗 Traffic-related pollution
- 🏗️ Construction dust
- 🔥 Local smoke events
- 🌫️ Seasonal pollution spikes

When pollution data is too coarse or delayed, authorities may not know where an emerging hotspot is until the situation becomes serious.
